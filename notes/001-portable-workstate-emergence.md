## Origin

The idea of a Portable Workstate emerged during cross-model continuity
experiments involving the AI Continuity Architecture Method.

The initial description of the project focused on an external **Memory Bank**:
a structured collection of context that could help an AI continue work across
separate sessions.

Further testing revealed a more precise distinction.

The goal was not necessarily to make one AI remember everything forever.

The goal was to preserve the state of the work so that another AI model could
continue from it.

---

## The Shift in Understanding

The original question was:

> Can an AI remember a complex project across sessions?

The more precise question became:

> Can a deliberately constructed project state be transferred to another AI
> model so that meaningful work can continue?

This shifted the focus from permanent AI memory to portable project continuity.
